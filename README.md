## How to read the list

**Free?** refers to the listing, not whether the product being submitted has a free plan. Any required payment, reciprocal link, or paid upgrade should be disclosed.

**Dofollow?** records the observed outbound link or a clearly labeled publisher claim. Use `Unknown` until checked. Link attributes can vary by plan and placement.

**DR** means Ahrefs Domain Rating. Use `—` when no dated measurement is available; do not substitute another authority metric.

**Best for** is an editorial assessment: `Users`, `Backlink`, or `Both`. It is not a promise of traffic or rankings.

**Last checked** is the date a contributor reviewed the submission route and listing terms, in `YYYY-MM-DD` format. Weekly automated checks test link availability; they do not refresh this date or prove that submissions are accepted.

## Maintenance

[Link checks](https://github.com/mmmmingos/where-to-submit-your-startup/actions/workflows/links.yml) run every Monday, on README/contribution-guide changes, and on demand. Each run provides a report in its summary and downloadable artifacts. A failed check needs review: a login wall or bot block does not necessarily mean the directory is dead.
